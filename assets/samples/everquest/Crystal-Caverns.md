# Crystal Caverns — unique loot

Velious dungeon (**~1.47×** on era servers). Hunt range **25–45**. Ry`Gorr orcs up top, Froststone Coldain in the middle, stalag terrors / spiders / geonids below. **Froststone dwarves have no loot** — leave them (Citizens of Froststone).

## Quest NPCs

Froststone is the Coldain remnant in the middle of the zone. Killing anyone there tanks **Citizens of Froststone** and they drop nothing. Erdarf Restil (Sceptre / Karsin Acid turn-in) is in **Thurgadin**, not here.
