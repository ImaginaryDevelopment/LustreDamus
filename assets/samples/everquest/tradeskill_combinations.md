# Tradeskill combinations

Classic / Kunark / Velious, P99-style caps. A combine's success roll is **skill versus trivial** only. Race and class do not change that roll. They decide who is allowed, who can pass 242–250, who can make cultural armor, and who gains skill faster.

Skill-ups use the higher of **INT** or **WIS**. Smithing also counts **STR** (whichever of the three is highest). Fletching and poison making also count **DEX**.

Pass **200** in one skill and every other skill stops at 200. Class-locked skills are the exception people rely on (poison making can be 250 beside another 250). Treat tinkering the same until you confirm it on your server. Alchemy caps at 171 anyway.

## Best combo

| Tradeskill | Cap | Allowed | Best combo | Fastest skill-up | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blacksmithing | 250 dwarf / 242 others | ALL | **Dwarf Warrior** | Erudite or High Elf Enchanter, until the 242 cap | Only dwarves reach 250. STR counts, and Brellium plate is dwarven cultural. A high-INT caster skills faster early and then stops at 242 |
| Tailoring | 250 | ALL | **Erudite Enchanter** | Erudite Enchanter | Highest INT, and Erudites tailor the silk plate nobody else can make. Do not spend this 250 on a gnome if that gnome is your tinkerer |
| Baking | 242 | ALL | **Halfling Druid** | Erudite or High Elf Enchanter | No race-locked recipes. Halfling cleric/druid is the WIS baker people actually park. An enchanter skills faster and has no cultural baking |
| Brewing | 248 | ALL | **Dwarf Cleric** | Erudite or High Elf Enchanter | No race lock. Dwarf cleric is the Kaladim WIS brewer. Barbarian shaman is the other natural one |
| Fletching | 235 | ALL | **Wood Elf Ranger** | Wood Elf Ranger | DEX counts, and Feir'Dal cultural weapons/bows are the wood elf forge. Cap is low, so this is a fine second skill under 200 |
| Jewelcrafting | 250 | ALL | **Erudite Enchanter** | Erudite Enchanter | No cultural lock. Leave the gnome's one skill-past-200 for tinkering. This is the 250 you put on an erudite who is not your tailor |
| Pottery | 250 | ALL | **Dark Elf Enchanter** | Erudite or High Elf Enchanter | No cultural lock. Neriak is the pottery city. Any INT caster skills it the same |
| Alchemy | 171 | **Shaman only** | **Iksar Shaman** | Iksar Shaman | WIS skill-ups. Of shaman races, Iksar has the highest WIS, then barbarian, ogre, troll. Cap is 171, so it does not eat the 250 |
| Poison making | 250 | **Rogue only** | **Dark Elf Rogue** | Halfling or Dwarf Rogue | DEX counts. Dark elf is the poison rogue. Halfling and dwarf start with higher DEX if you only care how fast it skills. Can be 250 next to another 250 |
| Research | 200 | **NEC WIZ MAG ENC** | **Erudite Enchanter** or **Erudite Wizard** | Erudite Enchanter / Wizard | INT skill-ups. Erudites have the highest INT. Cap 200, so it never takes the slot past 200 |
| Tinkering | 250 | **Gnome only** | **Gnome Enchanter** | Gnome Enchanter | Nobody else can tinker. Enchanter is the gnome with the highest INT. Make this the gnome's skill past 200 |

Pick one skill past 200 per character. The usual split: dwarf warrior smiths, erudite enchanter tailors **or** jewelcrafts (not both at 250), gnome enchanter tinkers, dark elf rogue poisons, iksar shaman alchemizes, wood elf ranger fletches under 200.
