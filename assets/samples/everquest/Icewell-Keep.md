# Icewell Keep — unique loot

Coldain keep above Thurgadin (`thurgadinb`). Hunt **45–60+**; **Dain Frostreaver IV** is **RAID**. Enter from **Thurgadin**. Same **Coldain** faction rules as the city — councilors, royals, and Dain are the hits most players refuse.

**Faction warning:** Killing **Dain**, the **Seneschal**, **Chamberlain**, **councilors**, **royal guards**, or named Coldain officers tanks **Coldain** (and wrecks Thurgadin armor access). Marked **FACTION** below even when the loot is excellent. Only giants/animals with no Coldain hit are free **KILL**s.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`thurgadinb`). `#Dain_Frostreaver_IV` → **Dain Frostreaver IV**.

**Do** column:
- **KILL** — not a meaningful Coldain hit (or junk mobs)
- **FACTION** — good loot, but hits **Coldain** (skip unless you are dumping faction on purpose)
- **LEAVE** — no unique worth taking
- **RAID** — formed raid (**Dain** is also a massive Coldain hit)
