# Accursed Temple of Cazic-Thule — unique loot

Classic / Luclin temple (`cazicthule`). IdleQuest ZEM **1.28×** (classic Lost Temple sheets often cite **1.13×**). Hunt **50–60+** on IdleQuest — this is the **Accursed Temple** revamp, not the classic 19–45 Ravenscale pyramid. Enter from **The Feerrott**. Tae Ew lizards, shiverbacks, oozes/masses, raptors, piranhas. No AAs: nameds are group; **Avatar of Fear** is **RAID**-tier (~60).

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`cazicthule`). Mob names match IdleQuest display (`#Avatar_of_Fear` → **Avatar of Fear**, `#a_Tae_Ew_warmaster` → **a Tae Ew warmaster**). Classic P99 **Ravenscale** / Cenobite / Archon / Templar / Diviner / golem tables are **not** what IdleQuest spawns here — those are Lost Temple–era.

Sort **Named spawn** by **Mob** when tracking. **LEAVE** rows are quest NPCs.
