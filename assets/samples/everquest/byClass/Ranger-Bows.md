# Ranger — groupable bows

Classic / Kunark / Velious. **Group camps only** — no Trakanon raid, Vox, ToV, Sleeper warders, or War Bow of Rallos Zek. NO DROP is fine. Same-account swap still works.

Sort by **Item** or **DMG**. Filter also matches zone / camp.

**At 45:** farm **Runed Oak** / **Spectral** / **Talisen**, or Hole golems for **Underfoot**. **Exquisite** (smith) and **Huntsman** / **Gloomwater** are the late group chase once you can sit Kael / SG.
