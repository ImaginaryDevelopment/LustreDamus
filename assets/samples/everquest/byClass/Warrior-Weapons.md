# Warrior — weapons by tankScore

Classic / Kunark / Velious. **60-group camps only** on the group table — no Trakanon, Nagafen, Talendor, Overking, Venril, or Temple of Veeshan. Same-account NO DROP swap still works.

Sort by **tankScore**. The damage/delay figure is in the stats column so a weapon can be compared, and it is not part of the score. A high-ratio weapon with no HP, AC, or STA ranks under a slow stat stick.

## tankScore

| Metric | Formula | Ignores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tankScore | 1×HP + 3×AC + 6×STA | STR, AGI, DEX, resists, haste, procs, mana, damage, delay |
