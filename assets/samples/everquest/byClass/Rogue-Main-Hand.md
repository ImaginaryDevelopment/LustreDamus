# Rogue — groupable main hand

Classic / Kunark / Velious. **Piercing primary** for **Backstab** — that is what this sheet is for. Offhand / 1HS ratio weapons are a separate sheet later. **Group camps only** — no Trakanon, Vox, VP dragons, ToV, Tunare, Tormax, or Sleeper. NO DROP is fine.

Sort by **Item** or **DMG**. Filter also matches zone / camp.

**Rule of thumb:** higher **damage** boosts backstab harder than raw ratio; once you have a solid 10+ dmg piercer, chase **Ragebringer**. Until then, **Sebilite Croaking Dirk** / **Sarnak Backstabber** are the Kunark group goals.
