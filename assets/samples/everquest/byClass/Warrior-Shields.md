# Warrior — groupable shields

Classic / Kunark / Velious. **60-group camps only** — no Trakanon, Nagafen, Talendor, Overking, Venril, or Temple of Veeshan. Same-account NO DROP swap still works.

Sort by **tankScore**. Filter also matches camp / restrictions. Offense, AGI, resists, haste, and procs are not in the score. Secondary slot, shield only. One-hand weapons that can sit in the off hand are on the weapon sheet.

## tankScore

| Metric | Formula | Ignores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tankScore | 1×HP + 3×AC + 6×STA | STR, AGI, DEX, resists, haste, procs, mana |
