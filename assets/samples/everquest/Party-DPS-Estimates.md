# Party DPS estimates

Rough Classic / Kunark / Velious kill-speed guesses for named camps (Sebilis juggs, city nameds, etc.). Not a parser — just stacking assumptions.

A normal **party** caps at **6**. **ENC + BRD + 6 rogues** needs a **/raid** (8+ boxes). Raid buffs (Celerity, Clarity, songs, Tash) still land on the other raid groups — that is the point of the bigger pack.

## Assumptions

- Same gear band on the DPS boxes (groupable Kunark / early Velious).
- Someone else tanks / heals when the table says so, or the pack burns a single target that dies before that matters.
- **Haste** means spell / song haste on melee (Celerity, bard haste songs), not only worn. ENC + BRD together push melee toward the haste cap better than either alone.
- **Mana** for casters means Clarity (ENC) and/or bard mana song — potions exist but are not the plan.
- No charm pet DPS counted. No AE train math — single-target burn.
- Adds / pulls / mez matter for *uptime*; pure burn rankings ignore that unless noted.
- Raid XP split is **worse** than a party — see [Raid XP](Raid-XP.md). This sheet ranks **burn / camp speed**, not XP/hour.

## Rogue packs (party of ≤6)

| Comp | Burn speed | Sustained camp | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ENC + 5 ROG | Strong | Good | Most knives a party can field under Celerity. No bard songs |
| ENC + 3 ROG | Strong | Good | Same idea with room for tank / heal |
| BRD + 3 ROG | Strong | Strong | Song haste + pulls. Slightly weaker / fiddlier haste than Celerity |
| BRD + ENC + 4 ROG | Good | **Best (party)** | Full song + Celerity stack; only four knives |
| BRD + ENC + 2 ROG | Good | **Best (small)** | Cap haste / control; fewest knives |
| 4 ROG | Weakest | Weak | Fourth rogue rarely pays for missing real haste |

## Rogue packs (raid — buff sharing)

| Comp | Burn speed | Sustained camp | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BRD + ENC + 6 ROG (+ tank/heal → 10) | **Best overall** | Strong | Raid required. Six knives at stacked haste; ENC slow/mez + bard pulls |
| BRD + ENC + 6 ROG (8 total) | **Best overall** | Good | Same burn if the named dies before tank/heal matters |

**Why the 10-raid pack wins burns:** a party cannot hold ENC + BRD + six rogues. Max party knives under both supports is **four** (`BRD + ENC + 4 ROG`). The raid adds **two more fully buffed backstabbers** and keeps Celerity + songs on everyone in range. That is a large kill-speed jump on one target.

**XP caveat:** each kill pays ~**half** a full party-of-6 share in a 10-raid (see Raid XP). You need roughly **2×** the kills/hour to match that party’s XP/hour. Six stacked rogues often get there on **named / slow spawns**; on easy trash, two separate parties usually XP better.

| Comp | Burn speed | Sustained camp | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Same XP caveat as rogues: **faster burns**, **worse XP per kill**. Worth it for named loot camps; split parties for grind XP.

## Conclusions

| Goal | Comp |
| --- | --- |
| Fastest named burn (melee) | **Raid: BRD + ENC + 6 ROG** |
| Fastest named burn (casters) | **Raid: BRD + ENC + 6 WIZ** |
| Best party-sized melee camp | **BRD + ENC + 4 ROG** (or ENC + 5 ROG if you refuse a bard) |
| Best party-sized caster camp | **BRD + ENC + 4 WIZ** (or ENC + 5 WIZ) |
| XP / hour on trash | Stay in **parties** — do not raid for grind XP |
| Skip | All-DPS with no ENC/BRD haste or Clarity/Tash |

## Why the support boxes win

- **Melee:** attack-rate haste multiplies dual-wield and backstab frequency. Stacked Celerity + songs on six rogues beats four stacked or five Celerity-only in a party.
- **Casters:** Clarity is uptime; **Tash** is effective DPS. Six fed, Tashed wizards beat anything a six-box party can field.
- **Raid cost:** you pay the raid XP split to buy those extra buffed DPS seats — see [Raid XP](Raid-XP.md).
