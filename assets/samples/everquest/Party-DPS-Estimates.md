# Party DPS estimates

Rough Classic / Kunark / Velious **4-box** kill-speed guesses for named camps (Sebilis juggs, city nameds, etc.). Not a parser — just stacking assumptions.

## Assumptions

- Same gear band on the DPS boxes (groupable Kunark / early Velious).
- Someone else tanks / heals, or the pack burns a single target that dies before that matters.
- **Haste** means spell / song haste on melee (Celerity, bard haste songs), not only worn.
- **Mana** for casters means Clarity (ENC) and/or bard mana song — potions exist but are not the plan.
- No charm pet DPS counted. No AE train math — single-target burn.
- Adds / pulls / mez matter for *uptime*; pure burn rankings ignore that unless noted.

## Rogue packs

| Comp | Burn speed | Sustained camp | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ENC + 3 ROG | **Best** | Good | Celerity on three backstabbers. ENC slow / mez helps the tank |
| BRD + 3 ROG | Strong | Strong | Song haste + pulls. Slightly weaker / fiddlier haste than Celerity |
| BRD + ENC + 2 ROG | Good | **Best** | Haste stacked toward cap; best control. Down one knife |
| 4 ROG | Weakest | Weak | Fourth rogue rarely pays for missing real haste |

**Conclusions**

- Take **ENC + 3 ROG** to melt one target.
- Take **BRD + ENC + 2 ROG** when you want clean pulls and fully capped melee haste all session.
- Skip **4 ROG** unless haste is already solved (strong worn haste on everyone, or another box buffing).

## Wizard packs

| Comp | Burn speed | Sustained camp | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ENC + 3 WIZ | **Best** | Strong | Clarity + **Tash** on three nuke channels |
| BRD + ENC + 2 WIZ | Good | **Best** | Mana song + Clarity + pulls/CC. Down one nuke |
| 4 WIZ | Burst only | Weak | No Clarity → dry fast; no Tash → resists / weak hits |

**Conclusions**

- Take **ENC + 3 WIZ** to kill things.
- Take **BRD + ENC + 2 WIZ** to stay up all night.
- Skip **4 WIZ** unless it is a one-fight pot dump.

## Why the support boxes win

- **Melee:** attack-rate haste multiplies dual-wield and backstab frequency. Three hasted rogues beat four unhasted.
- **Casters:** Clarity is uptime; **Tash** is effective DPS (land rate + damage). Three fed, Tashed wizards beat four dry ones on any real camp.
