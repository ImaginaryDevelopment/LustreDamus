# Velious factions

The three main Velious faction choices are **Coldain** (dwarves), **Frost Giants** (Kromrif / Kromzek), and **Claws of Veeshan** (dragons). You cannot really max all three — giant kills raise Coldain and CoV while tanking Kromzek.

## Default path

Most players ally **Coldain + Claws of Veeshan** and grind giants in **Kael Drakkel**. That unlocks **Thurgadin** and **Skyshrine** shopping / ports / quests, and keeps Kael as the efficient faction camp.

Siding with the Frost Giants is worse for casual play: you kill Coldain, lock out Thurgadin and Coldain quests, and thrash the easy armor path. Kael access is nice, but maintaining Kindly Kromzek while also raiding **King Dain** or **King Tormax** is painful.

Target **Kindly or better** with Coldain and Claws of Veeshan. Expect Frost Giant standing to sit low from Kael combat — that is normal on the default path.

## The three sides

| Faction | City / access | Best grind | Armor / rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Coldain** | **Thurgadin** (+ Icewell) | Kill giants in **Kael**; giant helmet turn-ins | Easiest Velious armor progression (**Corroded** / Thurgadin class armor). Generally lower stats than CoV / Kael sets |
| **Claws of Veeshan** | **Skyshrine**; also helps **Plane of Mischief** | Kill giants in **Kael**; sneak behind Tormax's throne | High-resist **Unadorned** / Skyshrine armor; some BiS quests. Pieces from ToV east RAID |
| **Frost Giants** (Kromrif / Kromzek) | **Kael Drakkel** | Kill Coldain dwarves | High-AC **Ancient Tarnished** Kael armor (Ally Kromzek; pieces from ToV west RAID). Hard to keep if you raid Dain or Tormax |

## Practical notes

- **Kael giants** are the shared grind for Coldain + CoV. You do not need a separate CoV camp if you live in Kael.
- **Giant helmets** → Coldain turn-ins speed Thurgadin standing without extra travel.
- **Behind Tormax's throne** is a sneak / faction trick for CoV when you need a bump without a full Kael session.
- **Thurgadin armor** (Kindly Coldain + Corroded pieces + gems) is the groupable 50–60 path. **Skyshrine** and **Kael** armor are Ally + ToV RAID molds — see [Quest Gear](Quest-Gear.md) and the city sheets.
- Raid **King Tormax** (Kael) or **Lord Yelinak** (Skyshrine) heads feed cross-faction turn-ins; check the Kael / Skyshrine sheets before eating heads.
