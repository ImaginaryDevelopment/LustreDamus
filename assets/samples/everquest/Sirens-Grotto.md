# Siren's Grotto — unique loot

Velious underwater dungeon (`sirens`). ZEM **0.85×**. Hunt **45–60+**. Connects **Western Wastes** ↔ **Cobalt Scar**. Cold / wet: Enduring Breath + invis help; seahorses and icy servants **see invis**. Sirens are CLR / ENC — dispel, snare, high MR. East Temple and Dark Temple are the main siren camps; seahorse tunnels and ulthork/walrus dens fill the rest. No AAs: nameds are group; Dark Temple nameds can need a strong 55–60 group.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`sirens`). Mob names below match IdleQuest display names (`#Fellspine` → **Fellspine**, `#Mistress_of_the_Darkwater` → **Mistress of the Darkwater**). Skip IdleQuest-only custom ore/gem placeholder drops on some `#` nameds.

Sort **Named spawn** by **Mob** when tracking. No in-zone LEAVE quest NPCs — epic halves are kill targets.
