# Wakening Land — unique loot

Velious outdoor (`wakening`). Hunt **35–55+**. Forest between **Kael**, **Skyshrine**, **Cobalt Scar**, and the **Plane of Growth** portal. Geonids, holgresh, sifaye / drixie, fauns, panthers, frost / storm giants, and dragon **Wuoshi**. No AAs: outdoor nameds are **group**; **Wuoshi** / **Lantaric`Dar** / Scout Leader Plavo are **RAID**-tier.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`wakening`). Many Tunarean / giant script NPCs are **0%** on static tables. Skip empty-name / placeholder rows.

Sort **Named spawn** by **Mob**. **LEAVE** = Tunarean / Aghllsews quest NPCs and merchants.
