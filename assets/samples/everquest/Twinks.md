# Twinks — account-share NO DROP gear

For servers that let you move **NO DROP** between characters on the same account (shared bank / mule). Farm on a keyed 50–60, park the piece on the twink. Classic / Kunark / Velious.

**IdleQuest** stats where noted. Worn haste does **not** stack with itself — one worn haste source wins (usually the highest %). Spell / song haste stacks separately toward the haste cap.

## Mana regen (casters / hybrids)

Worn **mana regen** (+N per tick on the item stat) stacks across slots on classic rulesets. IdleQuest shows it as **manaregen** on the item row. **Clarity** clicks (mana recovery over time) are not the same stat — they do not replace a +mr necklace. **Fungal Regrowth** on the fungi tunic / staff is **HP** regrowth, not mana.

| Item | Slot | Why | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coin Depicting Innoruuk | Held | Click **Clarity** | Ground spawn, Howling Stones south; Charasis key path | NO DROP, **not** NO RENT — rare clarity click you can park on a mule. Also a key combine |
| Stein of Tears | 2HB | Mana +25 (pool, not +mr) | **Bile Sentinel** (Howling Stones north) | ALL, NO DROP. Pairs with +mr pieces between pulls |
| Pearl Encrusted Stein | Secondary | Classic worn **+2 mana regen** offhand | **Plane of Fear** (Cazic / pearl quest combine) | IdleQuest row has no manaregen field; classic caster twink stein. NO DROP |
| Crown of Narandi | Head | **Mana regen +2** | **Narandi the Wretched** (Great Divide ring war) | **RAID** Coldain event. Velious |
| Choker of the Wretched | Neck | **Mana regen +1** | Narandi event | Same farm as the Crown. Neck +mr; Signet stays on the finger |

**Signet** and **Tolapumj's Robe** (Must-get) add large **Mana +** pools but not worn +mr — stack them with the table above.

## Priority by role (account mule)

**Melee twink:** Fungi tunic → worn haste sash (Dragonborn > Flowing Black) → Fingerbone / Mask → Signet → JBoots until feet haste (Grey Suede) exists.

**Caster twink:** Tolapumj's Robe → Signet → Fingerbone → Circlet of Shadow (NEC/SK) → Fungi staff → **Choker of the Wretched** and **Crown of Narandi** when the account does the ring war → Pearl Encrusted Stein → Coin / clarity click → JBoots. Skip fungi tunic (INT casters can't wear it).

**Hybrid / priest:** Fungi tunic or best AC chest you can mule → Signet → Fingerbone → Crown of Narandi, Choker of the Wretched, or Pearl Encrusted Stein for +mr → haste sash when chest haste is not already worn.

## Farm notes

- **Sebilis** keys off Trakanon Idol — worth parking a keyed mule; Spore King + Tolapumj are both inside.
- **Howling Stones** needs Key of the Seers / cube path — Crypt Spectre sash and Embalming Fluid hoop are worth the key.
- **Di`Zok Signet** is free once Warmly — turn in Green Goblin Skin / Mt. Death Mineral Salts at Herald even while KOS; skins from Droga / Nurga / Frontier goblins.
- Raid haste (Grey Suede, Cloak of Flames / Piety) is optional; groupable sash + Tolapumj already carry most twinks.
- **Bags:** Treasure Hunter's Satchel and Kavruul's Mystic Pouch are both 10-slot / 100% WR NO DROP giants off dragons — park on every mule. Nagafen / Vox are the classic farms; Faydedar can drop either.
- **Concordance of Research:** farm Greater Lightstones off willowisps, turn in to **Mrysila** (−671, −170 North Karana) or **Smith Tv`ysa** (South Ro). One bag per research toon; cheap and account-shareable on mule-friendly servers.
