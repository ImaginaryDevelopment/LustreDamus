# Velketor's Labyrinth — unique loot

Velious dungeon (`velketor`). Hunt **40–60**. Crystal spiders / watchers / gargoyles / sentinels in the labyrinth; **Icepaw kobolds** deeper; Froststone Coldain near the mid camps. No AAs: most nameds are **group**; **Velketor** and **Lord Doljonijiarnimorinar** are **RAID**.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`velketor`). `#Named` rows are **0%** on the spawn table (script / PH / disabled chance) but keep their loot tables — camp the listed PH. Skip script dummies (`TerrorSpawner`, `one`…`nine`, `aerainph`, `aedispellph`, `You are`, …).

Sort **Nameds** by **Mob**. **Do** column:
- **KILL** — fine to kill for loot
- **LEAVE** — Froststone Coldain (junk cloth / vendor fluff)
- **RAID** — formed raid
