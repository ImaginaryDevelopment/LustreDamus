# Sleeper's Tomb — unique loot

Velious raid dungeon (`sleeper`). Unmodified XP (**1.00×**). Hunt **55–70+** (min level **46**). Enter from **Eastern Wastes**. No AAs: the whole zone is **RAID** — trash is raid trash; warders, ancients, and Kerafyrm are **RAID**.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`sleeper`). Warders, ancients, Master of the Guard, Final Arbiter, Progenitor, and **The Sleeper** (Kerafyrm) are **0%** on static spawn tables (script / event). Loot tables below still match IdleQuest. Kerafyrm has **no loottable** in IdleQuest. Skip Area*/StaticShout/warning stubs.

**Do** column:
- **KILL** — fine to kill for loot (raid trash / lesser nameds with live spawn %)
- **RAID** — formed raid (warders / ancients / end bosses)
- **LEAVE** — quest / fluff NPC
