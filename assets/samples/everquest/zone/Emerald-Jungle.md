# Emerald Jungle — unique loot

Kunark outdoor (`emeraldjungle`). ZEM **0.83×**. Hunt **30–50+** (**Severilous** higher). Jungle between **City of Mist**, **Trakanon's Teeth**, and the overgrown ruins. Gorillas, iksar raiders, plague / spur / char bones, Erollisi plants, and green dragon **Severilous**. No AAs: outdoor nameds are group; **Severilous** is **RAID**-tier.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`emeraldjungle`). Skip `Several large` / empty placeholders.

Sort **Named spawn** by **Mob**. **LEAVE** = Spirit Sentinels and scout quest NPCs.
