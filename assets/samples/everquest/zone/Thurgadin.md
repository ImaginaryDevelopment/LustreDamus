# Thurgadin — unique loot

Velious Coldain city (`thurgadina`). Hunt **~30–45** city / bank / quest hub (pair with **Icewell Keep** for the Dain RAID). Connects **Great Divide**. **Coldain** faction gates vendors, bank, and the **Corroded → Thurgadin class armor** turn-ins — most players should not kill Coldain here.

**Faction warning:** Almost every named dwarf, lorekeeper, initiate, and wolfmaster is **Coldain**. Auto-level / bad pulls can wipe months of armor faction. Prefer **Great Divide** / outdoor Kromrif for kills.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`thurgadina`). Mob names match IdleQuest display. Skip generic Guard / citizen spam.

**Do** column:
- **KILL** — safe / no important Coldain hit (rare in this zone)
- **FACTION** — has loot worth noticing, but killing hits **Coldain** (usually skip)
- **LEAVE** — vendor, trainer, or quest NPC; junk or no unique worth the hit
