# Iceclad Ocean — unique loot

Velious outdoor (`iceclad`). Hunt **25–40+** (giants / Lodizal higher). Boat / ice-floe zone: gnome pirate island, **Snowfang** gnolls, frost giant scouts, **Lodizal** the ice turtle. Links **Eastern Wastes** / **Great Divide** approaches and the Velious boat. No AAs: outdoor nameds are **group**; **Lodizal** is **RAID**-tier.

**IdleQuest** spawn/loot truth: [brynnb/idlequest-content](https://github.com/brynnb/idlequest-content) (`iceclad`). Skip script dummies (`# 6`, `no wood here`, `Seal Spawn`, empty rows) and most **0%** statics unless noted.

Sort **Named spawn** by **Mob**. **LEAVE** = Snowfang / pirate / boat quest NPCs and merchants.
