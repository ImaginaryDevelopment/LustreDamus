# Cleric — Strike Lifesteal

Official build. Defense, difficulty Easy. This is the Blessed Strike build. Divine Shelter makes that strike hit and heal extra targets. Numbers are the wiki build record as of 2026-08-18.

Blessing of Mortan. Five pieces. The hammer casts Blessed Strike.

| Pieces | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
