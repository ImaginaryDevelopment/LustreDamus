# Mercenary — Swift Thunder

Official Swift Thunder Slash build. Offense, difficulty Common. No set. Haste adds slashes. Numbers are the wiki build record as of 2026-08-18.

## Playstyle

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Job | Mercenary |
| Role | Offense |
| Difficulty | Common |
| Engine | Swift Thunder Slash hits 3 times and gains 1 slash for every 2 stacks of Haste. Pierce and lightning |
| Bar | 4 skills, 5 masteries, 2 runewords |

## Weapons

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Slots | Two weapon slots |
| What fits | Any weapon, including another job's |
| Skills | Swift Thunder Slash needs a sword or a dagger in at least one slot. The other slot can be the bow or the hammer |

| Item | Quality | Type | This slot |
