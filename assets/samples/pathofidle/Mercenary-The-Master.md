# Mercenary — The Master

Path of Idle: Old Gods Rising. Numbers are the wiki's level-1 skill lines and the item lines as of 2026-08-18. Skill damage grows with skill level. The sword's beam line is the item text, not a skill-level row.

This is the official Death's Wind shell with the set sword replaced, and both sword basics put on the bar so the beam actually fires.

## The sword

| Item | Quality | Split | Listed effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Master | Mythic sword | 80% physical attack, 20% elemental attack | Physical Attack +1%. While Healthy, a basic-skill cast fires a sword beam: slash equal to 60% physical attack. The beam ignores physical defense. Strength 6, Dexterity 6. No sockets |
| The Master | Legendary sword | 80% physical attack, 20% elemental attack | Physical Attack +1%. While Healthy, a basic-skill cast fires a sword beam: slash equal to 30% physical attack. The ignore-defense line is not on this version. Workshop upgrade into the mythic |

Healthy means current health above 70%. Reserved health above 30% means the hero is not Healthy even at full life, and the beam stays off.

## Seat

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Leather mercenary, behind the front seat | The beam only fires while Healthy. A plate warrior or a cleric holds the hits. This hero's job is to stay above 70% and keep casting |
| Reserved health at or under 30% | Above that, full life still does not count as Healthy |

## Skill bar

Both basics are required. Death's Wind is the payoff the set is built around. The official bar does not include Shockwave or Shadow Strike, so those two are the change that makes the mythic do work.

| Skill | Kind | Weapon | Base cooldown | Lv.1 hit | Why it is on the bar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shockwave | Basic | Hammers, swords | 0.9s | 30% physical attack blunt, 60% Strength slash. Also grants increased Strength | Each cast fires the beam and counts toward the 4-piece |
| Shadow Strike | Basic | Swords, daggers | 0.8s | 80% physical attack pierce, and 1 stack of Haste | Second beam. Faster base cooldown than Shockwave |
| Death's Wind | Talent | Swords, daggers | 6s, 84 mana, 2 m | 4 hits. 160% Strength slash and 80% physical attack pierce | The whirlwind the 4-piece casts again every 3 Shockwaves |
| Battle Cry | Talent | Any | 10s, 140 mana, 3 m | Heal equal to 90% physical defense. Allies gain 25% mastery skill damage for 6s, and immunity to Slow | The self-heal that puts health back over 70%. The heal scales with physical defense |
| Killing Aura | Talent | Any | 0s, 9 m | Allies gain increased physical attack, and crit against normal enemies | The beam is a percent of physical attack |

Base cooldowns above are the level-1 values. On these two basics the wiki's level rows keep that same cooldown, so Basic Skill Speed is what shortens them in play.

## Left off the official bar

| Skill | Why it is off |
| --- | --- |
| Death's Mark | Pierce mark. It does not cast a basic, so it does not fire the beam. The official four are Death's Wind, Battle Cry, Killing Aura, and Death's Mark. The mark is the one that comes off to make room |

## Masteries

Same five as the official Death's Wind build. Placement on the talent grid is rolled per hero.

| Mastery | Effects | What it does for this sword |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloodthirst | Kill health restore +60%. Crit vs bloodied targets +90%. Base skill speed +70% | More basic casts, so more beams, and a heal on kill that helps stay Healthy |
| Berserk | Dexterity +100%. Slash damage +100% | The beam is slash. Death's Wind's larger hit is also slash |
| Stalwart | Shield power +90%. All defense +80%. Physical attack +50% | Physical attack is the beam's scale. Defense feeds Battle Cry's heal and keeps health up |
| Fierce | Strength +60%. Crit value +80%. Health +80% | Death's Wind slash and Shockwave slash both scale with Strength. Health makes the 70% line easier to hold |
| Hamstring | Pierce damage +70%. Blunt damage +70%. A basic hit has a 30% chance to apply 1 stack of Slow | Shockwave's blunt hit and Death's Wind's pierce hit. This line does not add to the beam |

Mythic weapons have no sockets, so the runes cannot go on The Master. Sockets are on Common and Fine items, level 20 and up. The ring is one of five set pieces. With it off, the sword, amulet, torso, and gloves are still the 4-piece.
