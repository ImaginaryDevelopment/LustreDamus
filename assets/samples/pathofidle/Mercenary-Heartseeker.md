# Mercenary — Heartseeker

Official Heartseeker Lifesteal build. Defense, difficulty Common. Cassius's Wicked Deed. Numbers are the wiki build record as of 2026-08-18.

## Playstyle

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Job | Mercenary |
| Role | Defense |
| Difficulty | Common |
| Engine | Heartseeker stabs for pierce and heals from crit value. While Killing Aura is active, the 4-piece gives that stab a 40% chance to skip its cooldown |
| Bar | 4 skills, 5 masteries, 9 equipment, 1 runeword |

## Weapons

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Slots | Two weapon slots |
| What fits | Any weapon, including another job's |
| Skills | Heartseeker needs a sword or a dagger in at least one slot. Cassius's Bleeding Blade is a dagger, so the other slot can be anything |

## Set

Cassius's Wicked Deed. Five pieces. The dagger is the weapon that keeps Heartseeker usable.

| Pieces | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Heartseeker hits 3 additional targets |
| 4 | While Killing Aura is active, Heartseeker has a 40% chance to not enter cooldown |

## Skills

| Skill | Kind | Weapons | What it does here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heartseeker | Talent | Swords, daggers | 3 hits. Pierce equal to 100% physical attack, and a heal equal to 24% crit value, at level 1. Cooldown 4 seconds, 56 mana. At level 10 the pierce is 255% physical attack and the heal is 61% crit value |
| Killing Aura | Talent | Any | Allies gain physical attack, and crit against normal enemies. The 4-piece cooldown skip applies only while this aura is active. It reserves health |
| Battle Cry | Talent | Any | Heals the caster from physical defense. Allies gain mastery skill damage and Slow immunity |
| Banner of Struggle | Talent | Any | Plants a battle flag. Allies gain a shield per second and 1 stack of Mettle. Shield power is 30% physical attack at level 1, up to 77% at level 10. Lasts 3 seconds, up to 5 allies. Cooldown 12 seconds, 168 mana |
