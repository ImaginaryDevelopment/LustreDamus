# Cleric — Holy Light

Official Holy Light Buff build. Defense, difficulty Easy. Chastise shortens Holy Bolt, and Holy Bolt heals and buffs. Numbers are the wiki build record as of 2026-08-18.

## Playstyle

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Job | Cleric |
| Role | Defense |
| Difficulty | Easy |
| Engine | Devotion of Faelen. Each Chastise takes 1 second off Holy Bolt. Holy Bolt grants allies 30% primary attributes for 3 seconds |
| Bar | 5 published skills, 5 masteries, no runeword. Chastise is the basic the 4-piece names, and it is not in those five |

## Set

Devotion of Faelen. Five pieces. The staff casts both Holy Bolt and Chastise.

| Pieces | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Allies hit by Holy Bolt gain 30% additional primary attributes for 3 seconds |
| 4 | Each Chastise reduces Holy Bolt's cooldown by 1 second |
