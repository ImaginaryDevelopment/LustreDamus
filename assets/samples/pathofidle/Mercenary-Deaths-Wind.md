# Mercenary — Death's Wind

Official build. Offense, difficulty Common. Shockwave casts trigger extra Death's Wind. Numbers are the wiki build record as of 2026-08-18. The Master sheet is the same set with the mythic sword in place of Yasuo's Deathblade.

## Playstyle

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Job | Mercenary |
| Role | Offense |
| Difficulty | Common |
| Engine | Yasuo's Gale. Every 3 Shockwave casts trigger another Death's Wind. Death's Wind always hits, and nearby targets take up to 100% increased damage |
| Bar | 4 published skills, 5 masteries, 1 runeword. Shockwave is the basic the 4-piece counts, and it is not in those four |

## Set

Yasuo's Gale. Five pieces. The sword casts Death's Wind and Shockwave.

| Pieces | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Death's Wind always hits, and deals up to 100% increased damage to nearby targets |
| 4 | Every 3 casts of Shockwave trigger an additional Death's Wind |
